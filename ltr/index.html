FAQ

Every question, answered

Is the MyWallet app free?

The base version is completely free. Pro features such as unlimited reports and cloud sync are available with the MyWallet Plus subscription.

Is my banking data safe?

Yes. Data is stored encrypted and only on your device; no sensitive information is ever kept on a server.

Which devices are supported?

Android 8+ and iOS 13+. The interface is also optimized for tablets.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Account → Manage subscription. You can cancel anytime, with no penalty.
